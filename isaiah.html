Focus Themes

The four things that matter

This is an important developmental window for building explosive movement qualities — if the reps are clean, consistent, and recovered well.

01

Striker movement off the ball

The hardest skill in the position — not finishing, but creating the chance by losing your marker. Drop deep to draw the center-back, spin behind. Stay onside. Time the run to arrive at the ball, not before it.

02

Finishing under pressure

First-time finishes, finishes after a touch, finishes with a defender closing. Decide your finish BEFORE the ball arrives — don't think with the ball at your feet. Side foot to placement, laces to power.

03

CMF — receive between lines

When you drop to midfield, the move is to find the gap between the opposition's midfield and defense, receive on the half-turn, play forward. Through balls weighted to runners. Scan twice before every touch.

04

Bounds, depth drops, sprint mechanics

Maximum CNS expression. Bounds for horizontal power, depth jumps for reactive strength, sprint mechanics for top speed. This is a good age to develop explosive movement qualities — if mechanics, recovery, and consistency are handled well.

Training rationale

At 13, the developmental window is open for combined plyometric + change-of-direction work to build explosive qualities — vertical jump, broad jump, and acceleration tend to respond well in this age band. With consistent high-quality reps in the 90-120 foot contacts per session range, improvements should become visible over the training block.

Before depth jumps unlock · readiness gates

Depth jumps go onto the menu in week 4. Age alone doesn't unlock them — landing quality does. Every item below must be true on the morning of the W4 Monday session. If any one fails, hold the depth jump out that week and reassess the next session.

✓ Broad jump landings are quiet — no audible slap, no double-bounce
✓ Knees track over toes — no inward collapse on landing
✓ Single-leg landings from a 6-inch step stable for 2 seconds
✓ Deceleration sticks (Thursday block) clean — no trunk lean
✓ No current knee, hip, heel, or back pain — even mild
✓ Posture stays tall by the third set of any Monday block — no fatigue collapse

Striker movement vocabulary

Goals come from movement before they come from finishing. Build a full library of runs and learn when each one is on. Drill them in the synergy blocks so they become instinct, not decisions.

RUNS · near-post run · far-post peel · check-away then check-to · pin the defender then spin · double movement (fake one way, go the other) · layoff then run beyond
FINISHES · finish across goal · finish near post · first-time finish · weak-foot (left) finish

Position Drill Library

Drills that are uniquely yours

Striker patterns + central midfielder patterns + the explosive work that's specific to your age and development window.

STRIKER · MOVEMENT

Drop + spin run

Drop deep toward midfield as if to receive. Center-back follows. As the pass is played, spin off the defender's shoulder and accelerate into the channel. Time the run with the ball — first step is the key.
3 sets · 6 reps · alternate shoulders
STRIKER · FINISH

1-touch finishing from crosses

Nora and Silas alternate crosses from either flank. One touch only — decide your finish before the ball arrives. Near post = side foot inside; far post = side foot back across. Score 5 in a row to advance.
3 sets · 8 crosses each flank · 60s rest
STRIKER · 1V1

Breakaway vs Eli

Start 25 yards out, dribble at goal vs Eli's set position. Read his first move — if he stays big, go round; if he commits, finish low and hard. Practice both finishes equally.
3 sets · 5 reps · alternate sides
CMF · RECEIVE

Between-lines half-turn

Stand between two cones (representing opposition lines). Server plays in. Scan before — open your body, first touch into space behind the line, second touch is a forward pass. Both shoulders.
3 × 10 reps · alternate scan direction
CMF · THROUGH BALL

Weighted through ball

From central position, play a through ball to Nora or Silas making a timed run. The pass has to split the gap — too soft = caught, too hard = past them. Disguise: look away, then thread it.
10 reps each foot · target gates
EXPLOSIVE · DEPTH WORK

Depth jump → finish

Only if readiness gates pass. Start at a 12-inch box — progress height only if landings stay quiet, posture holds, and no pain appears. Step off, hit the ground reactively, immediately sprint 5yd and finish a one-touch shot from a rolling ball. If any gate fails, replace with snap-down landing → 5yd sprint → finish.
2-3 sets · 3-5 reps · very-high tier · 12in to start · stop at first sloppy landing · gate every session
LEFT FOOT · CORE

Left-foot finishing block

From 10-12 yards, left foot only into a 2yd gate. Inside foot for placement, laces for power. Build to left-foot finishes after a right-foot setup touch — the natural cut from the right wing.
Across the full session · log accuracy weekly
STRIKER · HOLD-UP

Back-to-goal lay-off

Receive with your back to goal, shield with body, lay off to a runner (Nora or Silas). Don't turn — your job is to hold and release. Then spin and run beyond for the return ball.
3 × 8 reps · alternate runners
