Focus Themes

The four things that matter

These are the threads running through every session — what you're trying to develop with each rep, not just complete.

01

Cutting inside off the right

Your winger threat changes by side. On the left wing, the in-cut onto your right foot is the dangerous move — set the defender up wide, then explode inside on the diagonal for the shot or through ball. On the right wing, drive the line for the cutback, with the left foot threatening inside to keep the defender honest.

02

Left foot mastery

Left-foot work runs through the whole session — not just a single block. Some lives in ball mastery; the rest is built into passing patterns, 1v1s, crossing, and finishing. Slowly closing the gap with the right. Track juggling PRs weekly.

03

Scan-receive-turn for CMF

When you drop into CMF, the move is: head up before the ball arrives, open body shape, first touch into space. Make the second pass before the first defender arrives.

04

Sprint posture & arm action

Foundational running mechanics — tall posture, knee drive, opposite arm. At 10, this is the highest-leverage technical skill to lock in before adolescent growth spurts.

Training rationale

At your age, low-intensity plyometrics — jump rope, ladder hops, line jumps — can develop speed and jumping qualities without the joint load of higher-volume work. Sixty foot contacts per session is the ceiling, and the quality of every single landing matters more than the count.

Winger actions · by side

Your job changes depending on which flank you're on and where you are. Know the menu for each position so your decisions are instant — as a right-footed player, the two wings ask for different things.

RIGHT WING · drive the line, cutback · use the left foot to threaten inside and keep the defender honest
LEFT WING · cut inside onto your right foot · shoot, slip a pass, or combine
CENTRAL MID · scan before receiving · take the half-turn · switch play or play forward

Position Drill Library

Drills that are uniquely yours

Position-specific drills layered on top of the family session — winger and CMF technical work that develops your two-position skill set.

WINGER · IN-CUT

Cut-inside finish

Start wide on the left. Dribble inside on the diagonal, sell the outside lane, cut inside onto the right foot, and finish across the keeper. Progression: repeat from the right wing by driving the line first, then using a cutback or left-foot inside threat so defenders can't overplay the outside route.
3 sets · 6 reps · alternate flanks · 45s rest
WINGER · 1V1

Wide channel beat

5yd × 15yd channel. Beat the defender either outside (down the line) or inside (cut to shot). Read the defender's hips — if they over-commit, take the opposite. End every rep with a cross or shot.
3 sets · 4 reps each side · 60s rest
CMF · RECEIVE-TURN

Pre-scan + half-turn

Server plays into your feet. Before the ball arrives, scan over your shoulder once. First touch opens your body to the play. Second touch passes to a target. Done at speed, this becomes automatic.
3 × 10 reps · both shoulders · server alternates direction
CMF · THROUGH BALL

Weight + disguise

From central position, play a through ball to a runner timing their move. Practice the weight (too soft = intercepted, too hard = past the runner) and the disguise (eyes elsewhere, look at the runner late).
10 reps each foot · alternate runners
WINGER · CROSS

Driven low cross

Drive to the byline, plant, low driven cross with laces — across the face of the goal, between 6-yard box and penalty spot. Aim for a 3ft corridor. Both feet over the 8 weeks.
3 sets · 8 crosses · alternate flanks
LEFT FOOT · CORE WORK

Left foot · woven through the session

Left-foot reps appear everywhere, every session — not boxed into one block. Anchor reps: wall passes (3 × 20), short-range strikes (3 × 5 into a gate from 8yd), dribbling sequences with the left only. Then keep using it in patterns, 1v1s, and finishing. No right-foot rescue.
Across the full session · log juggling PR weekly

Off the Field

The other 23 hours

The 60-minute session is only as good as what surrounds it. Your version of the off-field work, tuned to a serious 10-year-old.

Your essentials

SLEEP · ~9-12 hrs, consistent times — the biggest lever you have, and it's free
REFUEL · real food with carbs + protein within an hour of training, especially after the hard days
FILM · ~15-20 min/week of your own games — look for width, depth, support, and when to dribble vs. release the ball. Skip 11v11 systems for now
JUGGLE · 5-10 min daily · LEFT foot · target 10 → 25 by W8 · 2 PB attempts per day
