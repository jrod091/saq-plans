Focus Themes

The four things that matter

A keeper's development is its own discipline. These are the four lanes you're working in for the next 8 weeks.

01

Footwork & set position

Every save starts with feet. Shuffles to stay set, crossovers to cover ground, micro-adjustments to get the body behind the ball. The shot-stop is the last 0.3 seconds — the footwork is the 3 seconds before it.

02

Diving progression

Three-stage build: collapse dive (from set, fall to ball), step-save (one step, then dive), step-power (two steps + explosive jump). 8 weeks to clean and extend all three.

03

Distribution & left foot

Modern keepers play with their feet under pressure. Your right is solid — your left is where the project lives. Throws, rolls, side volleys, drop kicks, both feet. Every session.

04

Daily mobility

Hip, shoulder, and thoracic spine range of motion is the single most leveraged investment a keeper can make in dive amplitude and durability. Ten minutes every day — not optional, not skippable.

Training rationale

At 15, you're in the developmental window where the full progressive plyometric menu is appropriate — vertical drops, hurdle hops, depth jumps, reactive bounds — provided mechanics and recovery are handled well. Combined with position-specific stimuli (visual reaction triggers, lateral starts), this is a strong stimulus. Daily 10-minute mobility work should support better positions, cleaner dives, and more comfortable range over the course of the program.

Daily Mobility Checkpoint

10 minutes. Every day. No exceptions.

This is the highest-leverage habit in your entire program. Built into Tuesday's session as a dedicated block, but the goal is daily — including weekends and rest days. Three zones, three minutes each, with one minute to breathe and reset between.

  • HIPS — 90/90, pigeon, couch stretch
  • SHOULDERS — sleeper, doorway pec, CARs
  • T-SPINE — open book, quadruped rotation
Diving safety · non-negotiable rules

Diving and depth-to-save patterns are the highest-stress drills in your week. These rules are mandatory for the full 8 weeks and beyond. If any of them gets violated, the rep doesn't happen.

! Diving ONLY on safe grass, turf, or padded mat — never on hard ground
! STOP immediately if wrist, shoulder, hip, back, or groin pain appears
! NO max-amplitude dives when fatigued — regress to step-save or collapse
! BOTH sides must be trained evenly — log left/right reps
! Diving and depth-jump-to-save days are NEVER stacked — 48h minimum between
! Distribution volume monitored — tight hip or groin = reduce drop kicks that day
! Broad-jump-to-dive ONLY when landing mechanics are clean and surface is safe

Position Drill Library

The goalkeeper menu

Eight blocks covering handling, footwork, diving, crosses, 1v1s, distribution, reactive work, and mobility. Rotate based on the day-type — Monday hits power, Tuesday hits mobility, Thursday hits reactive, Friday hits distribution.

HANDLING · FUNDAMENTALS

Basket / diamond / W-grip

Three catch positions, three techniques. Basket = low (hands cupped, pinkies together). Diamond = high (thumbs together overhead). W-grip = chest-driven shots (thumbs behind, fingers spread). Smooth, never snappy on contact.
3 × 10 each technique · server kneels 6y away
FOOTWORK · SET POSITION

Shuffle & set rhythm

3 lateral shuffles, set feet shoulder-width, hands at hip height, knees bent. Server tosses on the set. Resets to opposite direction. Trains the rhythm of arriving SET, not arriving running.
3 sets · 8 reps each direction · 30s rest
DIVING · STAGE 1

Landing mechanics

Before any diving, learn to LAND. Lie on side. Hands soft, side of leg first, hip bone protected, top arm wraps. Then: kneel and fall to the side. Then: standing collapse onto the side of the leg. Builds the falling pattern that protects shoulder, hip, wrist.
3 × 6 reps each side · slow tempo · W1-2 · safe surface only (grass/turf/mat)
DIVING · STAGE 2

Collapse dive

From set position, fall sideways onto the ball (placed on the ground 4 feet to your side). Lead with the hand closest to the ball. Soft landing on the side of the leg, not the hip bone. Now you're combining the falling pattern with ball contact.
3 × 6 reps each side · slow tempo · W1-3 · safe surface only · both sides logged
DIVING · STAGE 3

Step-save

Server rolls/tosses the ball 6 feet wide. One explosive step with the closest foot, then dive. Land on side, hands first to the ball. Both sides equally. Builds the step pattern that makes saves repeatable in a game.
3 × 5 reps each side · 45s rest · W2+ · safe surface · stop on any joint pain
DIVING · STAGE 4

Low-amplitude power dive & reactive step-save

Two steps + jump dive at moderate amplitude — ground first, not hip-first. Ball thrown firmly 7-8 feet wide. Then progression: server adds late direction call so the dive is reactive, not planned. Both sides equally.
3 × 4 reps each side · 60s rest · W3-5 · safe surface · no max amplitude when tired
DIVING · STAGE 5

Advanced extension & depth-to-save

Step-power dive with extended reach. Then advanced: step off a 12-inch box, hit ground reactively, immediately set and save a thrown ball. Only when stages 1-4 are clean, only on safe surface, only when fresh. Max-amplitude attempts are occasional — single-digit reps, only when completely fresh, never when form is dropping. The depth-to-save pattern is the highest-stress combo in your week — never stack on a max-distribution day.
2 × 3 reps each side · 90s rest · W4+ · VERY HIGH tier · gates + safe surface · single digits · max amplitude only when fresh · never stacked with depth-jump day · never fatigued
CROSSES · DECISIONS

Claim vs punch

Sibling crosses from either flank. Decision: can you catch cleanly? Claim with two hands at the highest point. If contested or under pressure, punch with both fists toward the touchline. Communicate every call.
3 sets · 6 crosses each flank · alternate decisions
1V1 · POSITIONING

Set / narrow / spread

Three responses to the 1v1. SET = stay big, force the shot. NARROW = step forward, take the angle. SPREAD = star-fish dive low at the ball when committed. Isaiah runs at you from 25y — practice all three responses.
3 sets · 4 reps each technique · long recovery
DISTRIBUTION · BOTH FEET

Throw / roll / side volley

Target gates 25, 35, 45 yards out. Throws (overhand and side), rolls (low ground), side volleys (out of hands). Both hands, both feet. Score 6/10 to advance distance. Left foot side volleys are the project.
3 sets · 10 reps each technique · log accuracy
REACTIVE · TRIGGERS

Reaction ball block

6-sided reaction ball dropped from above. Catch it on the first bounce. Then with verbal triggers from server ("LEFT!" / "RIGHT!") — react and shuffle before the ball is tossed. Trains the visual + auditory processing.
3 × 12 catches · 20s rest
REACTIVE · POST-PUBERTY

Depth jump → save

Only if Stage 1-4 dive progression is clean, readiness gates pass, the surface is safe, and you're fresh. Start at a 12-inch box — progress height only if landings stay quiet and form holds. Step off, hit the ground reactively (no pause), shuffle 3 feet, set, save a thrown ball. If any condition fails, replace with a step-save or collapse dive.
2-3 sets · 4-5 reps · 90s rest · W4+ · very-high tier · 12in start · single digits · never stacked with diving day
MOBILITY · DAILY

Hip / shoulder / T-spine

90/90 hip transitions, sleeper stretch, doorway pec, scapular CARs, open-book thoracic rotations, pigeon. Three minutes per zone, one minute breathing reset between. Every. Day.
10 min · daily · slow controlled tempo
FOOTWORK · LADDER

Lateral + set finish

Lateral 2-in through ladder, exit into GK set position, catch tossed ball, reset. Combines the ladder footwork with the position-specific application. Both lead feet.
3 sets · 6 reps each lead · 30s rest

Off the Field

The other 23 hours

At ECNL RL + Varsity, the off-field work — especially film — is genuinely high-leverage for a keeper. This is where a lot of goalkeeping develops.

Your essentials

SLEEP · ~8-10 hrs, consistent times — CNS recovery for all the reactive work
REFUEL · carbs + protein within an hour; monitor hip/groin tightness given the diving and distribution volume
FILM · ~30 min/week, the most valuable of the four — positioning and angles, starting position vs. through-balls, organizing and commanding the back line, cross management, distribution decisions. Own games + a pro keeper to model
